# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Build a multi-page website for Launchpad Studio, a creator-focused digital marketing studio, featuring separate pages for services, pricing, demo store with product detail pages, process explanation, testimonials, and consultation booking.

**Planned changes:**
- Create 7 separate page routes: Home, Services, Launch Packages/Pricing, Demo Store, How It Works, Testimonials, and Contact/Book a Consultation
- Build Home page with business overview, testimonial preview, and CTAs for consultation booking and viewing packages
- Create Services page listing 5 service categories: brand identity, website development, ecommerce setup, digital product launches, and creator monetisation strategy
- Build Launch Packages/Pricing page with 3 tiers using example pricing in ₹ INR (₹1,999, ₹4,999, ₹14,999)
- Create Demo Store page with product grid showcasing Creator Hoodie, Monetisation Ebook, and Online Course/Digital Template
- Build individual product detail pages for each Demo Store product with image, description, ₹ INR pricing, and Buy Now/Enquire Now CTA
- Create How It Works page with 4 process steps: Brand Strategy & Planning, Website and Store Setup, Product & Merch Launch, and Monetisation & Growth
- Build Testimonials page with client testimonials including name, role, and feedback in card or slider layout
- Create Contact/Book a Consultation page with booking form and contact details
- Implement modern, premium, creator-focused design with consistent typography, clean layouts, and professional footer across all pages
- Ensure every page includes prominent CTA buttons linking to relevant pages or forms
- Use sophisticated color scheme conveying professionalism and premium quality

**User-visible outcome:** A professional multi-page website where users can navigate between separate pages to explore Launchpad Studio's services, view pricing packages in ₹ INR, browse a demo store with clickable products that open detailed product pages, understand the service process, read testimonials, and book consultations.
